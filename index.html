Battery Alert Pro

Info

About

Battery Alert Pro is a modern utility for Windows 11 and Windows 10 designed to help you protect and manage your laptop's battery health. By performing customizable actions when your battery is too low or too high, this tool helps you avoid overcharging and deep discharging, extending the lifespan of your battery. It runs silently in the background with no ads.

Features

Features

  • Low & High Battery Alerts to prevent over-draining and overcharging.
  • Customizable Actions: Choose from a looping sine-wave beep, playing a custom MP3, running a batch script, or a visual screen alert.
  • Timed Actions: Set a duration for any alert, after which the action (including scripts) is automatically terminated.
  • "Finally" Actions: Automatically put your computer to sleep or shut it down after an alert duration expires.
  • Smart Hysteresis: Prevents constant, annoying alerts once a threshold is crossed. Includes a manual re-arm button for full control.
  • Autostart with Windows so your battery is always protected.
  • Silent 7-Day Trial with secure, hardware-bound RSA licensing.
  • Lightweight & Efficient: Runs silently in the system tray with minimal resource usage.
REQ

Requirements

  • Operating System: Windows 10 or Later
  • Framework: Microsoft .NET Desktop Runtime.
    (If this is not already on your system, Windows will automatically prompt you to download and install this free component from Microsoft when you first run the application.)
